Interview with Amel, 3rd-year business student (StageFinder discovery, week 2).
"I spent three weeks looking for an internship. Offers are scattered across Facebook groups, company sites and emails from the faculty. I never know if a posting is still open."
"I would use an app if it shows only offers for my field and tells me the deadline. I don't want to create yet another account, honestly."
"Companies rarely reply. When they do, it's after two or three weeks, and I've already accepted something else."
"Paying? Maybe a few dinars a month, but only if it really saves me time. Students don't have much money. A free version with alerts would be fine for me."
